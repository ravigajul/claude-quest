    How to play

    Fix Dana's broken tests before 5:00 PM.

    You play Claude, the AI. Dana is the developer who asked for help. You have 12 minutes.

    1. Investigate. Each card is a tool Claude can use: grep a log, read a file, ask Dana. Cards show you raw evidence. They cost minutes and fill your memory tank.
    2. Work it out yourself. When you have enough evidence, Diagnose the root cause, then choose a fix. Wrong calls cost time and Dana's trust.
    3. Prove it, then 5:00 PM. Run the tests to win. If time runs out, the day restarts and you keep only 3 notes. Your first day is meant to fail.
